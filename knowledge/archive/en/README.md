# The Wise Tech

## Vision
The Wise Tech exists to give back to society by helping people use technology responsibly. We focus on improving quality of life and simplifying interactions so that people can invest their time in relationships with friends, family, and communities.

## Core Ideas
- **Purposeful Technology:** Digital solutions must serve people, never the other way around.
- **Simplification:** Technology should remove friction and reduce cognitive load.
- **Quality of Life:** Every platform or tool we build should measurably enhance well-being.
- **Human Connection:** We intentionally design experiences that minimize distractions and nurture meaningful interactions.

## Foundational Principles
We evolve software technology through progressive, incremental stages that preserve what we have already learned. This mindset relies on three pillars:

1. **Process Improvement** – Continuously refine how we build, release, and operate software.
2. **Knowledge Capitalization** – Capture lessons learned so future teams can build from a stronger baseline.
3. **Resilience Behavior** – Design for failure, graceful recovery, and long-term adaptability.

## Self-Awareness and Reflection
Consider how you relate to technology depending on the role you play today:

### Software Technology Consumers
- **Impact on Relationships:** How does technology influence your ability to stay connected with loved ones?
- **Simplicity vs. Complexity:** Does technology ease your life or add more layers of stress?
- **Mindful Use:** Where could a more intentional use of technology improve your daily routines?

### Software Developers
- **Code Quality and Impact:** What long-term effects do your decisions have on end users and maintainers?
- **Innovation vs. Stability:** How do you balance experimentation with the need for dependable systems?
- **Collaboration and Learning:** Are you sharing knowledge and leveraging past projects to improve the next one?

### Platform Engineers
- **System Resilience:** Are your platforms designed to handle failures and adapt to change?
- **Scalability and Efficiency:** Can the platform grow without sacrificing performance or sustainability?
- **User-Centric Design:** How does your work improve accessibility, reliability, and ease of use for others?

Reflect on these prompts to identify opportunities where your actions can align more closely with The Wise Tech principles.
