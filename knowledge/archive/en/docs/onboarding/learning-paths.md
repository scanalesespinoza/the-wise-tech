# Learning Paths for aDevelopment Teams

Use these modular paths to accelerate proficiency. Mix and match based on your role and current maturity.

## 1. New Product Engineer (Week 1–2)
- Read [Onboarding Tour](tour.md) and watch repository walkthrough recording.
- Pair with mentor to review one historical PR that exemplifies Essential alignment.
- Complete "Hello Observability" lab: add structured logs + metrics to a sandbox service.
- Shadow a release to observe checklist usage and narrative changelog creation.

## 2. Platform/DevOps Engineer (Week 1–3)
- Audit existing automations against [DocOps Workflow](../the-wise-tech-adevelopment.md#83-documentation-generated-from-code).
- Implement one improvement to CI (linting, security scan) and document it in [Changelog Guidelines](../changelog-guidelines.md).
- Set up dashboard for Essential principle breaches (timeouts missing, failed contracts).
- Co-create a recipe with a product engineer to capture platform expectations.

## 3. AI-Augmented Reviewer (Continuous)
- Memorize prompts in Section 4 of the playbook and adapt them to current repositories.
- Validate AI-generated PR summaries for accuracy and add clarifying questions.
- Curate false positives/negatives into the [Glossary](../glossary.md) and recipes.
- Host monthly clinics showcasing best reviews and how AI assisted learning.

## 4. Mentor/Lead (Quarterly)
- Facilitate Essential principle review workshops and update ADRs if needed.
- Track KPIs from Section 6 and share trends during retrospectives.
- Evaluate onboarding feedback and refine [Learning Paths](learning-paths.md) accordingly.
- Sponsor at least one experiment that automates knowledge capture (doc-gen, recipe mining).

Adapt the steps as your team grows. The goal is to make learning compounding and inclusive through shared resources and AI-augmented practice.
