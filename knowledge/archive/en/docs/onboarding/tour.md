# Onboarding Tour: Navigate The Wise Tech Repository

Welcome! This guided tour highlights the most relevant assets so you can contribute confidently within your first week.

## 1. Understand the Vision
- Read [`en/README.md`](../../README.md) to grasp mission, principles, and language structure.
- Review [Essential Principles](../principles-essential.md) to know the guardrails that define quality.

## 2. Learn the Delivery Model
- Study [The Wise Tech Approach](../../wise-tech-approach.md) to contrast our mindset with traditional pipelines.
- Explore the [Symbiotic Process Diagram](../../symbiotic-process-diagram.md) for an end-to-end view of collaboration.

## 3. Adopt the aDevelopment Playbook
- Walk through [The Wise Tech + aDevelopment](../the-wise-tech-adevelopment.md) focusing on sections 4–8.
- Note automation examples and how AI assistants augment reviews, documentation, and retrospectives.

## 4. Apply Practical Guides
- Check the [PR Checklist](../checklist-pr.md) before opening your first change.
- Read at least two [recipes](../recipes/README.md) relevant to your domain (errors, observability, testing, resiliency).

## 5. Prepare Operational Evidence
- Familiarize yourself with [Changelog Guidelines](../changelog-guidelines.md) to document impact.
- Update the [Glossary](../glossary.md) when you encounter new terms or acronyms.

## 6. Connect with Mentors
- Identify the domain mentor listed in the PR template and schedule a 30-minute sync.
- Share questions asynchronously in the team channel using the checklist as conversation starter.

## 7. Deliver Your First PR
- Clone the repo, run automations locally, and follow the template prompts.
- Request feedback on clarity of documentation and how well you tied the change to Essential principles.

Complete these steps and you will have the context needed to contribute code, docs, and operational improvements aligned with The Wise Tech philosophy.
