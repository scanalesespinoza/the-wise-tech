# Architect teams before platforms

The talk "Architecting Open Source Teams" argues that platforms cannot thrive if the people behind them lack shared purpose, guided autonomy, and meaningful recognition. For Wise Tech, this view complements our principles by showing that continuous improvement flourishes when simple processes protect human energy and knowledge is shared systematically.

## Key ideas
- **Human diagnosis first.** Before selecting tools, understand motivations, skills, and learning pace to avoid investments without adoption.
- **Directed freedom.** Clear purpose, defined boundaries, and constant feedback build resilience without sliding into bureaucracy.
- **Multifaceted recognition.** Celebrate technical excellence, mentorship, community impact, and continuous improvements to sustain commitment.
- **Operational transparency.** Open reviews, peer mentorship, and visible merit reinforce human connection.
- **Learning culture.** Continuous feedback and small visible wins maintain collective momentum.

## How to apply it
1. **Map capabilities and goals.** Run a quarterly inventory of team skills and aspirations before locking platform roadmaps.
2. **Set boundaries and freedoms.** Define shared objectives, success criteria, and spaces for autonomy in each initiative.
3. **Establish feedback rituals.** Schedule short, frequent sessions that surface collaboration signals and friction points.
4. **Highlight contributions.** Maintain a lightweight board that showcases mentorship, improvements, and reusable learnings.
5. **Document and share lessons.** Turn them into guides, templates, or purposeful automation that compounds collective knowledge.

## Link to Wise Tech
This approach strengthens continuous improvement and resilience by prioritizing people over tooling, promotes simplicity through lightweight structures, and accelerates knowledge capitalization with visible documentation and mentorship. Above all, it preserves human purpose and connection by sustaining an ongoing loop of feedback and recognition.
