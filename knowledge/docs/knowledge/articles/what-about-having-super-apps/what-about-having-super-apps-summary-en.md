# What about having Super Apps?
**Source:** https://sergio-canales-e.medium.com/what-about-having-super-apps-c1e70255caef  
**Author:** Sergio Canales Espinoza  
**Date:** 2024-03-05  
**Extraction:** structured-summary (faithful representation, no full copy)

## Central idea
The article reinterprets “Super Apps” as applications that master resilience capabilities required in distributed architectures. Rather than relying on magical frameworks, each service must manage states (operational, degraded, paused) with detection and response actions. Organizations should consolidate collective lessons to turn those capabilities into a baseline.

## Key points (bullet list)
- Decentralization forces every microservice to embed recovery and containment mechanisms.
- Critical capabilities include observability, state management, and proactive/reactive actions.
- Without a clear reference, teams must define minimum capabilities inspired by distributed system challenges.
- A Super App maintains operational peace, withstands attacks, and capitalizes on adverse experiences.
- Building foundational capabilities is the prerequisite before chasing advanced labels.

## Argument structure
1. Analyzes frustrations with modern architectures and lack of internal capabilities.
2. Details the states and transitions every unit of work must manage.
3. Concludes by proposing the Super App notion as an expression of organizational resilience.

## Relevant data/examples
- States: working, degraded, paused; transitions guided by observed conditions.
- References to microservices patterns and 12-factor apps as starting points, not complete solutions.

## Limitations/scope
- Offers no concrete implementation examples; needs technical adaptation.
- Aimed at teams experienced with microservices and cloud operations.

## Practical conclusion (≤120 words)
Building Super Apps means standardizing resilience capabilities in every service: monitoring, controlled degradation, and automated response. Turning incidents into institutional knowledge and sharing it strengthens the entire system.
