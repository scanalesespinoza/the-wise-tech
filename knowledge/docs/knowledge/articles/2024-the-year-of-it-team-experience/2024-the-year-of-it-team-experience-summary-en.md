# 2024: The Year of IT Team Experience
**Source:** https://sergio-canales-e.medium.com/2024-the-year-of-it-team-experience-858b7fc564d7  
**Author:** Sergio Canales Espinoza  
**Date:** 2024-01-20  
**Extraction:** structured-summary (faithful representation, no full copy)

## Central idea
The author argues that IT team experience will be the decisive factor in 2024. Market maturity demands accessible tools that let teams “do more with less,” shifting investment from maintenance to customer value. The key is prioritizing Developer Experience, Team Onboarding, and Platform as a Product to reduce operational load and multiply impact.

## Key points (bullet list)
- The market seeks to expand technological capability while simplifying its use.
- Accessibility depends on how many people are needed to leverage the technology.
- Leading teams redirect investments from maintenance toward direct customer value.
- Experiences such as DevEx, UX, and managed platforms determine competitiveness.
- The historical goal is clear: friendly tools that deliver more value.

## Argument structure
1. Describes the evolution of technology from complexity to mass accessibility.
2. Emphasizes that team and user experience will be the competitive differentiator.
3. Concludes by urging leaders to prioritize people-centered tools.

## Relevant data/examples
- References to the transition from laboratory computing to personal devices.
- List of key concepts: Developer Experience, Team Onboarding, Platform as a Product.

## Limitations/scope
- No specific metrics; needs complementary indicators.
- Strategic message aimed at technology and platform leaders.

## Practical conclusion (≤120 words)
Boosting team experience means automating operations, freeing capacity for innovation, and measuring satisfaction continuously. Investing in platforms that lower barriers and enable sustained value delivery ensures competitive relevance.
