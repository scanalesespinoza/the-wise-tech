# 🛠️ Architecting Open Source Teams: Building People Before Platforms
**Source:** https://sergio-canales-e.medium.com/%EF%B8%8F-architecting-open-source-teams-building-people-before-platforms-ceb88e0906f6  
**Author:** Sergio Canales Espinoza  
**Date:** 2025-04-13  
**Extraction:** structured-summary (faithful representation, no full copy)

## Central idea
Building open source teams inside companies depends more on human architecture than on technology. Initiatives start as sparks of collaboration and only endure when they become essential work, supported by lightweight structures, visible recognition, and directed freedom. The article describes how to balance autonomy and accountability to scale without losing the project’s soul.

## Key points (bullet list)
- Successful projects begin with spontaneous conversations and genuine willingness to help.
- Survival requires connecting to real objectives and delivering immediate value.
- Lightweight structures—clear guidelines, simple contribution paths, shared vision—keep momentum.
- Recognition is part of the architecture; demos, docs, and discussions deserve visibility.
- “Directed freedom” blends autonomy with enough support to lead and stay on course.

## Argument structure
1. Introduces the initial spark behind internal open source efforts.
2. Explains how to move from enthusiasm to organizational sustainability.
3. Details practices for scaling without bureaucracy and closes with enabling leadership.

## Relevant data/examples
- Suggested rituals: monthly reviews, celebrations of diverse contributions.
- Structural elements: single collaboration guide, reiterated vision, room to explore.

## Limitations/scope
- Provides no hard metrics; requires additional KPI design.
- Geared toward corporate environments with emerging collaborative culture.

## Practical conclusion (≤120 words)
Architecting open source teams means ensuring shared purpose, simple processes, and constant recognition. When people come first, platforms become sustainable and aligned with real outcomes.
