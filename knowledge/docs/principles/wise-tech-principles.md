# Wise Tech Principles

## Simplicity
Prioritize the smallest effective solution to keep teams focused and adaptable.

## Mejora continua
Iterate in small, observable steps that compound into lasting capability.

## Resiliencia
Design for graceful failure, fast detection, and intentional recovery loops.

## Capitalización del conocimiento
Capture insights in reusable artifacts so every team can build on them.

## Propósito humano
Align technology decisions with the outcomes people need the most.

## Conexión humana
Encourage transparent collaboration and feedback rhythms across roles.
