# Wise Tech Approach

Wise Tech combines human judgment with AI-assisted loops. Teams start with clear intent, codify guardrails, and continuously re
view telemetry to adapt. This approach leans on lightweight rituals, observable feedback, and shared ownership across roles.

- **Intent first:** Define the human outcome and the smallest experiment that proves value.
- **Augment, do not replace:** Use AI tooling to accelerate analysis, testing, and documentation without compromising trust.
- **Close the loop:** Instrument workflows, review learnings weekly, and capture deltas in ADRs and playbooks.
